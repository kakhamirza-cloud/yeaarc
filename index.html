faq

What are ARC mfers?

5,000 newly generated stick-figure PFPs built from CC0 mfers trait layers — randomized combos (not remaps of original token IDs), with an Arc navy backdrop. First mfers collection on Arc Chain.

Is this stolen art?

No. Original mfers by sartoshi are public domain (CC0). ARC mfers are a generative derivative: new trait combinations composited from those free layers, not a background swap of existing mfers.

Which network?

Arc Chain. Your wallet will switch if you’re on the wrong network.

How do I mint?

Open the mint page, connect your wallet, and wait for the contract announcement on X.
