    how to play

    tutorial

    • ← Left and Right → climb the ladder — mash either side as fast as you want.
    • Collect ⚡ Speed Boosts for an instant +2 lines.
    • Dodge 💥 Obstacles — getting hit stuns you for 2 seconds.
    • Rare 🎡 Wheel tokens can appear at a random height of 150+ (e.g. 150, 200, 300, 412 — max 1 per run). Grab one and a spin wheel shows up after the game — 1% Free Mfer Arc, 30% Whitelist, or 69% Zonk. Skip it and you lose the chance.
    • Each tap moves you up one ladder line.
    • Climb as high as possible before the ⏱ 30s timer ends.
    • Leaderboard resets every Sunday — flex your score before it wipes.

    Desktop: ← and → arrow keys (any mix is fine)

    Mobile: big ← → buttons at the bottom
