    how it works

    Quick start
    1. Enter with any wallet.
    2. Claim the daily faucet (+500 test points).
    3. Pick Yes or No on a market — one bet per market.
    4. Wrong side = 0. Right side shares the pool.
    Farm points

    Test points are for this desk. Later they convert to real points at about 100:1 (100 test → 1 real — final rate TBA). Farm now so you’re ready when rewards go live.

    Want more points?

    Share with a friend (opens a tweet). You get a share bonus once per day — spam clicks won’t stack.

    Shop

    The shop is for ARC mfers only. Everyone can play the prediction markets; holders unlock the points shop later.
